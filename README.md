Certalys

Infrastructure Intelligence for people who run infrastructure.

Certalys is an Infrastructure Intelligence Platform focused on providing
a clear, traceable view of IT infrastructure and its operational state.

The platform is being developed for heterogeneous environments and for
people who operate infrastructure — from ambitious HomeLabs to
professional IT environments.

What Certalys stands for

-   Clarity — relevant infrastructure information should be
    understandable and focused on operational decisions.
-   Traceability — results should remain connected to their origin and
    supporting information.
-   Provider neutrality — infrastructure should not be viewed through
    the boundaries of a single vendor ecosystem.
-   Digital sovereignty — operators should retain control over their
    infrastructure, their data and their technological decisions.
-   Privacy and data protection — operational data is handled
    deliberately and protected as part of the product design.
-   Quality before speed — stability, maintainability and validated
    behavior take priority over rushed feature delivery.
